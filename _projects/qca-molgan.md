**Quantum machine learning for molecular discovery · IEEE QAI 2025**

Molecular generation requires balancing several desirable properties. QCA-MolGAN explores a hybrid approach that couples a molecular GAN with a **quantum circuit Born machine** as a learnable latent prior.

The quantum prior is trained to align with features learned by the discriminator. A **multi-agent reinforcement learning** component guides generation using drug-likeness, lipophilicity and synthetic-accessibility objectives.

The work connects my research in quantum generative modelling with molecular discovery at **QunaSys**.

### Paper

*QCA-MolGAN: Quantum Circuit Associative Molecular GAN with Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning*. Aaron Mark Thomas, Yu-Cheng Chen, Hubert Okadome Valencia, Sharu Theresa Jose and Ronin Wu.
