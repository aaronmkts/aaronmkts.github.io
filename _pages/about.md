I am a **quantum machine learning researcher**, a PhD student in Computer Science at the **University of Birmingham**, and a research intern at **QunaSys**. My supervisors are **Dr. Sharu Jose** and **Dr. Ronin Wu**.

My research explores how quantum and classical models can learn useful representations and generate new data, with a particular focus on **generative modelling, generalisation and molecular discovery**. I am also interested in **generative AI and agentic AI**, and how these methods can support scientific research.

At Birmingham, I am part of the [Science for AI & AI for Science group](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/research/centres-institutes/research-in-computer-science/science-for-ai-ai-for-science). At QunaSys, I work on quantum machine learning for drug discovery, contributing to the QuEnAIS and FULL-MAP collaborations.

My work spans quantum generative adversarial networks, the reliability of graph generators, and efficient adaptation of language models. I previously researched graph machine learning at the **National Institute of Informatics in Tokyo** and sparse language-model fine-tuning with **DeepWok at Imperial College London**. Before my PhD, I completed an MSci in Theoretical Physics at Imperial.
