## Research experience

### QunaSys · Research intern
**October 2024–present · Copenhagen, Denmark**

I work on quantum machine learning for drug discovery, contributing to the QuEnAIS and FULL-MAP European collaborations. My work explores hybrid quantum–classical generative methods for drug and battery discovery, including [QCA-MolGAN]({{ '/projects/qca-molgan/' | relative_url }}).

### National Institute of Informatics · Research intern
**October 2025–April 2026 · Tokyo, Japan**

I worked on graph machine learning and co-authored [Same Graph, Different Likelihoods]({{ '/projects/graph-calibration/' | relative_url }}), investigating how different sequential encodings of the same graph affect an autoregressive model's likelihood estimates. The work appeared at the AISTATS 2026 workshop on calibration for modern AI.

### DeepWok, Imperial College London · Research intern
**June–October 2023 · London, United Kingdom**

I researched parameter-efficient fine-tuning of language models, co-authoring [Refining Salience-Aware Sparse Fine-Tuning Strategies for Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.1541/), published as an ACL 2025 long paper.
